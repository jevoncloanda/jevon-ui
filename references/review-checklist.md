# Product-fit review

Apply only relevant items. Use concrete evidence and user impact rather than taste scores. Impeccable can provide detailed design or technical audits; this checklist checks Jevon UI's responsibilities.

- **Authority:** Does the result honor user requirements, project specifications, scoped references, and intentional conventions in the documented order?
- **Surface:** Does a product screen support real tasks, information, and exceptions? Does a brand/portfolio surface retain appropriate expression?
- **Density:** Can operational users scan and compare enough information without excessive decoration or whitespace? Are consumer/touch experiences spared inappropriate enterprise density?
- **Preservation:** Were current screens, tokens, components, typography, spacing, borders/radii, responsive behavior, and interactions inspected and fitting patterns reused?
- **Scope:** Are changes confined to the requested outcome and its responsible layers, without unrelated screens, library replacements, dependencies, or design-system rewrites?
- **References:** Was each reference inspected, scoped, adapted, and compared in the rendered result? Are inaccessible evidence and interaction assumptions explicit?
- **Defaults:** Do conspicuous cards, pills, gradients, charts, and headings serve a reason? Were explicit aesthetics retained rather than vetoed by a generic catalog?
- **Composition:** Does the primary user task visually dominate? Do unrelated sections have unjustified equal weight or dimensions? Does every card or nested surface serve a real purpose, or would a compact row, divider, or inline tip work better? Is a large container holding very little content, and is its empty space intentional or caused by rigid sizing? Does the result feel tailored to this application?
- **Responsive hierarchy:** Does composition preserve content roles at smaller widths, keeping utility hints compact and functional content usable, rather than just stacking oversized desktop cards?
- **Data disclosure:** Is this another representation or deeper information? Could the chart provide an accessible entry point? Does the chosen toggle, drawer, detail view, or compact disclosure fit the task, data size, and frequency, reduce effort, and preserve context? Are labels specific and supporting data real, rather than a repeated dashboard template or simulated drill-down?
- **Completeness:** Do changed interactions and relevant states work, with accessibility preserved and important table comparisons/actions available at supported widths?
- **Coordination:** Was Impeccable selected for a concrete need, or sensibly skipped? Does the final result still fit project-specific authority?
- **Proof:** Were relevant rendered screens, viewports, interactions, and available states inspected, visible defects fixed, and fixes confirmed? Are repository checks complete and missing proof stated precisely?
